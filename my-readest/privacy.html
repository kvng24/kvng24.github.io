Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

My Readest is a personal e-book reader app. When you connect Google Drive, it asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and change only the files it creates itself. It cannot read any other file in your Drive.

What it stores in your Drive

Inside a Readest folder in your own Drive: your books, covers, reading progress, highlights and notes, and your reading record for the reading companion. These files stay in your Drive and are used only to keep your own devices in step.

What it does not do

It does not send your Google data to any server run by the developer, does not sell or share it, and does not use it for advertising. Google sign-in tokens stay on your device.

The reading companion

The reading companion uses outside AI services with the user's own keys: passages of the book being read and parts of the reading record go to Anthropic's Claude API to write notes, questions and suggestions, and spoken answers go to Deepgram for transcription. These requests come from the app on your device, not from a developer server.

Removing access

Disconnect Google Drive in the app's settings, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the Readest folder from your Drive at any time.
